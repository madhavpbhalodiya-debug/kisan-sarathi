About Kisan Sarathi

Kisan Sarathi is a digital platform designed to provide useful agricultural information and services to farmers. It helps farmers access information about agriculture, government schemes, farming services and other useful resources from one place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make agricultural information and digital services easier for farmers to access. Kisan Sarathi aims to provide a simple and farmer-friendly digital experience.
